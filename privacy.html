Privacy policy

Activity Tracker is a private app used by a single family. It is not available to the public.

What the app collects

On the family's own Windows PCs, the app records which program is in the foreground, its window title (unless excluded), and for how long. Titles matching the parent's exclusion list are replaced with "[hidden]" before being saved.

Google user data

The app uses the Google Drive permission drive.file, which only lets it see and change files the app created itself. It uses this to upload the usage files and a summary report to a Google Drive account owned by the family. It does not read, change or delete any other files in that Drive.

No data is sent to the app's developer or to any third party. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Storage and removal

Data stays on the family's PCs and in the family's own Google Drive. Removing the app, deleting its data folder, and deleting the ActivityTracker folder in Google Drive removes all data. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: contact the family member who installed the app.
